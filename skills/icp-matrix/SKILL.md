# ICP matrix

Use the target project's current product and commercial authority. This portable method does not set Build n Bloom's market, price, guarantee or revenue bands. The earlier NDIS agent catalogue is preserved in [the archive](../../archive/2026-09-27/INDEX.md) and must not be treated as a current qualification rule.

## Inputs

- Current offer, buyer, market priorities, delivery constraints and explicit exclusions.
- Evidence of the buyer's problem, existing way of working and material operational or commercial consequence.
- Decision owner, workflow owner, permitted sources and acceptance requirements.
- Evidence of demand and ability to purchase; record unknowns rather than assigning invented thresholds.

## Build the matrix

For each segment, record the job, trigger, buyer and affected users, current alternative, available evidence, delivery fit, decision process and disqualifiers. Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses. Do not route a buyer to an agent catalogue before understanding their work.

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Problem and consequence | A specific observed constraint or buyer statement, with date/source |
| Scope | A bounded outcome the current offer can serve |
| Owner and decision | Named roles responsible for purchase and acceptance |
| Data and access | Permitted inputs and ability to work within applicable obligations |
| Commercial fit | Buyer-specific budget/process evidence; no inherited example price |
| Readiness | Stable enough process, operators and review capacity |
| Next step | Proportionate discovery, standard product, method correction, scoped design or no-build recommendation |

## Qualification output

Return fit (`supported`, `hypothesis`, or `not currently supported`), evidence, unresolved questions, disqualifiers and the next useful check. One positive answer or a model-generated score is not an approval to install, contact, quote or contract. Existing client instruments keep their actual scope.
